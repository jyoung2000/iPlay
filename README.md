# jPlay

jPlay is a music discovery player for **Android (with Android Auto)** and **Windows**. Start from songs you like and it
builds a queue of full songs, blends them like a DJ, and learns your listening mood. The logo takes your mood's colour.

## Download
Get the latest build from [Releases](https://github.com/jyoung2000/iPlay/releases):

| File | Platform |
|---|---|
| `jplay-0.20.2.apk` | Android 8+ (and Android Auto). Installs over earlier Airwave Discovery releases and keeps your library. |
| `jPlay-0.20.2-x64-setup.exe` | Windows 10/11 installer |
| `jPlay-0.20.2-x64-portable.exe` | Windows portable app (no install) |

The signing certificates are self-signed, so Play Protect and Windows SmartScreen may warn on first install.

## New in 0.20.2
- Android Auto: radio filters (genre, saved places, language, starred), the station's name on the player, and quick EQ
  and Discovery buttons plus a Sound & discovery settings list.
- Customize discovery: genres and subgenres, artists and their circle, up to 100 songs, familiar or adventurous, older or newer.
- Volume up to 200%. Album pages load with a skeleton. Artist pages show frequent collaborators and similar artists.

## Upgrading from Airwave Discovery
jPlay is the new name for Airwave Discovery. Updating keeps your library, settings, favourites and downloads: on
first start the app moves its old "Airwave Discovery" folders to "jPlay" (nothing is copied, deleted or downloaded again).
